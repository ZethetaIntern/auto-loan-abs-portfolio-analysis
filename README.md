# Auto Loan ABS Portfolio Analysis – Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing the performance, risk, delinquency, cash flow, collections, recoveries, vintage behavior, prepayments, and credit risk of an auto loan portfolio.

The objective is to transform loan-level and portfolio-level data into meaningful business insights that can support portfolio monitoring, risk assessment, and management decision-making.

---

## Business Objective

The project focuses on answering key business questions such as:

- What is the current size and composition of the loan portfolio?
- Which regions and states have the highest portfolio exposure?
- How is loan delinquency distributed across the portfolio?
- How are defaults and losses changing over time?
- Which loan segments have higher delinquency rates?
- How effective are collections and recoveries?
- How does portfolio performance vary by vintage?
- What are the prepayment trends?
- How is the portfolio distributed across IFRS 9 stages?
- How much Expected Credit Loss (ECL) provision is associated with different risk segments?

---

## Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains six analytical dashboards:

### 1. Executive Portfolio Overview
Provides a high-level view of portfolio exposure, delinquency, vintage performance, portfolio balance, and loan segmentation.

### 2. Risk & Delinquency
Analyzes delinquency levels, DPD buckets, defaults, and portfolio risk characteristics.

### 3. Collections & Recovery
Focuses on collections, recoveries, losses, and collection performance.

### 4. Vintage & Prepayment Analysis
Analyzes vintage performance, cumulative defaults, cumulative net losses, pool factor, SMM, CPR, and prepayment behavior.

### 5. Cash Flow & Portfolio Performance
Analyzes portfolio cash flows, beginning and ending balances, scheduled amortization, losses, recoveries, defaults, collection efficiency, and prepayment trends.

### 6. Delinquency & Credit Risk Analysis
Analyzes IFRS 9 stages, PD, LGD, EAD, ECL provision, losses, recoveries, borrower characteristics, and delinquency status.

## Key Analytical Areas

### Portfolio Performance
Analysis of portfolio balance, loan counts, pool factors, and portfolio composition.

### Delinquency & Default Risk
Analysis of DPD buckets, delinquency status, defaults, and risk concentration.

### Collections & Recoveries
Analysis of collection efficiency, recoveries, gross losses, and net losses.

### Vintage Analysis
Comparison of portfolio performance across different origination vintages.

### Prepayment Analysis
Analysis of SMM and CPR to understand prepayment behavior.

### Credit Risk
Analysis of PD, LGD, EAD, and ECL across IFRS 9 stages and geographical segments.
